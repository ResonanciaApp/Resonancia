Create a deep, progressive, psychedelic Goa Trance journey that feels ancient, mystical, cosmic and profoundly immersive. The track should feel like entering an interdimensional temple in the middle of a desert under a sky full of stars, where sound becomes consciousness and rhythm becomes ritual.

Genre & Style:
Deep Goa Trance / Progressive Psychedelic Trance / Old-school Goa DNA with modern cinematic depth.

BPM:
138–145 BPM, hypnotic and steady, never too aggressive. The groove should feel fluid, organic and entrancing.

Mood & Emotional Direction:
Mystical, transcendental, deep, cosmic, tribal, euphoric, introspective, spiritual awakening, sacred and multidimensional. The music should induce a meditative trance state while gradually building intensity and emotional expansion.

Structure & Evolution:

Long atmospheric intro (1–2 min) with evolving textures, subtle drones and immersive sound design.
Slow and intelligent progression; avoid abrupt transitions.
Continuous layering and unfolding of sonic elements like a journey or ritual.
Build toward powerful psychedelic peaks without losing depth.
Multiple emotional phases: mystery → tension → expansion → transcendence → blissful release.
Epic ending with spacious cosmic ambience.

Bass & Groove:
Deep rolling psy bassline with organic movement and hypnotic repetition. Warm, rich and immersive low-end, subtle groove variations to maintain flow. The kick should feel deep and rounded rather than harsh or overly compressed.

Drums & Percussion:
Complex but tasteful tribal percussion, shakers, organic textures, distant toms, subtle ethnic rhythms, hypnotic syncopation and spacious stereo movement. Percussion should feel ceremonial and primal.

Melodies & Harmonics:
Classic Goa-inspired acid melodies mixed with deep atmospheric harmonic layers. Use evolving arpeggios, mystical scales, hypnotic synth lines and emotional harmonic progressions. The melodies should spiral and morph like consciousness expanding.

Sound Design:
Rich psychedelic textures, granular ambience, resonant sweeps, morphing synths, analog warmth, acid sequences, celestial pads, cosmic atmospheres, subtle field recordings of wind, distant thunder, sacred spaces, whispers or ritual ambience.

Psychedelic Elements:
Create intelligent psychedelic movement with stereo modulation, evolving delays, subtle glitch textures, resonant filters, spiraling sounds and dimensional transitions. Avoid cliché or cheesy psytrance sounds.

Instruments & Sonic Palette:

Analog Goa synth leads
Acid 303-inspired textures
Deep cinematic pads
Ethnic ceremonial percussion
Cosmic drones
Mystical choirs or sacred vocal textures (very subtle, ethereal, not dominant)
Organic ambient recordings blended naturally

Mix & Production:
Wide, immersive, cinematic stereo field. High-definition, warm analog feel with deep sub frequencies and sparkling highs. Spacious reverb but controlled. Mastered for deep listening, headphones and ceremonial dance environments.

References / Inspirations:
Inspired by the deep psychedelic storytelling of early Goa Trance, mixed with modern progressive psychedelic soundscapes. Think of a fusion between cosmic spirituality, sacred geometry, altered states of consciousness and ancient rituals translated into sound.

Important:
Avoid commercial EDM energy, cheesy festival drops, generic psytrance clichés, overly bright leads or aggressive compression. Prioritize depth, immersion, hypnosis, spiritual atmosphere and progressive evolution.

Final feeling:
The listener should feel like they traveled through a multidimensional portal, dissolved the ego, and returned transformed.
